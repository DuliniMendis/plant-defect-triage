- Original code written for this demo.

- Code, data, confidential material or brand assets (logos, typefaces, screenshots) belonging to anyone else.
- Any connection to a real maintenance or asset management system.

The equipment makes and models in the fictional fleet (for example Caterpillar, JCB, Toyota and Atlas Copco) are real products, named only to make the demo realistic. Their trademarks belong to their owners.

## Services used

- Vercel (hosting, Blob photo storage, AI Gateway).
- Neon Postgres, through the Vercel Marketplace.
- Anthropic Claude models (Sonnet 5.5, Haiku 5.5), through AI Gateway, to draft the issue and work order. Photos and notes sent for a draft go to the model provider.
- Chrome's built-in speech recognition for dictated notes, which sends the audio to Google for transcription.
